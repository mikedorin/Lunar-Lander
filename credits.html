How these were made

These are modern browser versions of classic programs. They were rebuilt in JavaScript with the help of AI coding assistants, working from the original program listings. Any bugs or differences from the originals belong to these versions, not to the original authors.

Sources

  • Hammurabi and Stock Market are based on BASIC programs published in David H. Ahl's Creative Computing books and magazine. The listings are collected at vintage-basic.net, where the original authors are credited in each listing. In 2022 Ahl placed his classic computing publications in the public domain.
  • Galactic Sweep is an independent, re-themed version of Super Star Trek, a classic BASIC game by Bob Leedom, based on the original Star Trek by Mike Mayfield, as published by David H. Ahl and collected at vintage-basic.net.
  • Football is a browser version of the 1978 BASIC game N.F.U. Football (No Fortran Used) ("N.F.U." is a joke about not using Fortran), published by David H. Ahl in Creative Computing and collected at vintage-basic.net.
  • Lunar Lander follows the physics of the 1969 FOCAL Lunar Lander program, as presented and documented by Jim Storer at Brandeis University.
  • Wireframe Front is based on cbzone, a vector tank game for X11. cbzone derives from Fortran sources by Justin S. Revenaugh and was translated and extended in C by Todd W. Mummert. Tank and scenery shapes are taken from the cbzone source.
